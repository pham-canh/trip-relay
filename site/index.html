Privacy receipt no boundary events yet

Every consent decision, peer fetch, blocked value, and denied tool on this page is appended to a hash-chained receipt. The log is redacted before hashing, so an email address or key never appears in it. It is kept in this browser tab's database, survives a reload, and is not sent to either site.
