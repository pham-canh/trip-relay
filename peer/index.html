The quay

A small hotel on a working harbour

Fishing boats unload under the windows at six, the cafes open at seven, and by evening the whole quay smells of grilled sardines. We keep the rest simple.

  • Breakfast on the terrace From 07:00 to 10:30, with bread from the bakery behind the fish market.
  • Boats at the door Ferries to the outer islands leave from the jetty in front of the hotel.
  • Bicycles to borrow Ride the coast road to the lighthouse and back before lunch.
  • Quiet after ten Thick walls, shutters that close properly, and a harbour that sleeps early.

Hotel Assistant

Our concierge travels, your details do not

Harbor Hotel publishes its concierge as an open agent network. A partner such as Skyline Air can run it inside its own visitors' browsers, on their router, after they approve. It receives a question, and answers it.

  • Shared with partners Two public reads, availability and the shuttle, through a published network that runs locally in your browser.
  • Never shared with partners Room requests and guest details. A partner site can only link here, where you enter them yourself.
